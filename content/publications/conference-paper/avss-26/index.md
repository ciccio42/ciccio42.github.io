abstract: 'In the Industry 4.0 paradigm, automated quality inspection based on Artificial Intelligence is increasingly adopted in manufacturing to prevent defective products from reaching consumers, while improving efficiency and reliability. In this context, the inspection of mattress components represents a particularly challenging application due to the variability of possible production defects. Here, anomaly detection emerges as a promising solution, as it is well suited to handle defect variability without requiring exhaustive examples of all imperfection types. Nevertheless, existing visual inspection solutions still mainly rely on supervised learning approaches, which are constrained by the need for extensive labeled data and the lack of domain-specific benchmarks. To address these gaps, we propose MIRAGE (Mattress Inspection through integrated Reasoning and Anomaly Grounded Embeddings), an end-to-end visual inspection pipeline leveraging the unsupervised pre-trained and cross-modal representational capabilities of Vision-Language Models (VLMs). To mitigate the lack of specialized data, we present a novel benchmark comprising over 7,800 frames of highly heterogeneous normal and anomalous mattress side bands. Extensive experimental evaluations demonstrate that MIRAGE outperforms both zero-shot VLM and state-of-the-art CNN baselines, confirming to be a viable solution for mattress inspection.'
