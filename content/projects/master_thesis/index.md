During my thesis, I undertook a challenging project intending to evaluate state-of-the-art
data-driven model-free methods for solving the complex 6D Grasp Pose Estimation
Problem and implementing them on a real robotic system running on an embedded
platform.
To achieve this, I proposed a repeatable test procedure to evaluate and compare the
ability of such systems to predict grasping poses that a real manipulator can perform.
My thesis work involved designing the software for the execution of pick-and-place
operations, using the ROS framework, and implementing and integrating the designed
modules covering the detection and execution pipeline.
