During my Bachelor's degree thesis, completed as part of the Apple Foundation Program, I
designed and implemented an automatic irrigation system for a smart pot based on the
MCU ESP-32. In addition, I designed and implemented a data transmission system using
Bluetooth Low Energy and MQTT protocols to enable communication with an iOS
application. To complete the project, I also designed and developed an iOS application to
facilitate user interaction with the smart pot.
